# Week 2 — Building Blocks (Days 8–14)

> **Theme:** Complete your chord set, get your strumming steady, and give your voice real notes to sing. Start separating what your hands do from what your mouth does.
>
> **Chords this week:** G (easy and full), Dm, plus a review of Em and Am
> **Voice this week:** 5-note scales, pitch matching, echo singing, singing along with the record
> **Together this week:** counting, talking and humming while strumming; your first loop of the song's progression

### Week 2 goals
By Day 14 you can:
- [ ] Play **G** (full `320003`, or easy `xx0003` as a fallback) cleanly
- [ ] Play **Dm** (`xx0231`) cleanly
- [ ] Change between **any two** of Em, Am, G, Dm at **20+ per minute** (Em ↔ Am at **30+**)
- [ ] Strum eighth notes (D U D U D U D U) steadily at 70 BPM
- [ ] Count "1 2 3 4" out loud while strumming without stopping
- [ ] Talk (about anything) while strumming 4 downs per bar
- [ ] Sing a 5-note major and minor scale in tune (confirmed on a recording)
- [ ] Hiss steadily for **20+ seconds**
- [ ] Play the song's basic loop **Am → Em → G → Am** in time at 60 BPM

**Session template:** [Week 2 template in 06 — Daily Routines](06-Daily-Routines.md#week-2-template-35-min) (~35 min)

## Day 8 — G chord
**Date:** Fri Oct 2 · 🌙 post-shift: 30-minute cap

**Time:** ~35 min · **Reading:** [01 — Guitar Fundamentals §9 (G)](01-Guitar-Fundamentals.md)

**Warm-up (10 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body + Breathing
- [ ] Voice Warm-up A

**Guitar (18 min)**
- [ ] Spider (all strings)
- [ ] One-Minute Changes: Em ↔ Am. Log it
- [ ] Learn **full G** (`320003`): finger 2 on string 6 fret 3, finger 1 on string 5 fret 2, finger 3 on string 1 fret 3
  - Thumb low behind the neck, wrist pushed slightly forward under the neck, fingers arched
  - Pluck-check all 6 strings. The usual problems: finger 1 touching string 4, or finger 2 touching string 5 (muting it)
- [ ] If full G is impossible today: learn **easy G** (`xx0003`, strum strings 4–1) and use it this week. Spend 3 minutes a day on full G shape-building anyway
- [ ] Lift-and-land G ×20
- [ ] Strum G, 4 downs, ×10
- [ ] Slow changes: **Em → G** ×10, **G → Am** ×10 (take your time)

**Voice (5 min)**
- [ ] **Voice Warm-up B, first time:** 5-note **major** scale on "mah," starting low and moving up by half steps, 5 repetitions. Use a piano app or a "5 note scale" video to follow along

**Record & Review (2 min)**
- [ ] Record G: pluck-check + strum

**Done when:** you can form G (either version) and get most strings ringing.

## Day 9 — G changes & eighth-note strumming
**Date:** Sat Oct 3 · 🌙 post-shift: 30-minute cap

**Time:** ~35 min · **Reading:** [01 — Guitar Fundamentals §10, 11 (Pattern 3)](01-Guitar-Fundamentals.md)

**Warm-up (10 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body + Breathing (log your hiss)
- [ ] Voice Warm-ups A + B

**Guitar (18 min)**
- [ ] Chord Check: Em, Am, G
- [ ] One-Minute Changes: **Am ↔ G** and **Em ↔ G** (new pairs!). Log both
- [ ] **Eighth notes (Pattern 3):** metronome 60, strum Em **D U D U D U D U**, counting "1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &." Downs on numbers, ups on "&." Keep it even. 3 minutes
- [ ] Ups only hit the top 3–4 strings. That's correct
- [ ] Downs slightly louder than ups
- [ ] Same on Am, then on G

**Voice (5 min)**
- [ ] **Pitch match:** pluck strings, hum and sing them on "ah." Then try this: strum Am, then **pluck just the A string** and sing it. Can you still find the note after strumming the whole chord?
- [ ] **Echo singing (new!):** on the A string, play open → fret 2 → fret 3 (A, B, C). Sing it back. Then fret 3 → 2 → open. Then make up your own 3-note patterns

**Record & Review (2 min)**
- [ ] Record eighth-note strumming on Em at 60 BPM

**Done when:** you have logged scores for Am ↔ G and Em ↔ G.

## Day 10 — Dm chord
**Date:** Sun Oct 4 · 🌙 post-shift: 30-minute cap

**Time:** ~35 min · **Reading:** [01 — Guitar Fundamentals §9 (Dm)](01-Guitar-Fundamentals.md)

**Warm-up (10 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body + Breathing
- [ ] Voice Warm-ups A + B (add the **minor** 5-note scale on "noo" today)

**Guitar (18 min)**
- [ ] Spider
- [ ] One-Minute Changes: Em ↔ Am, Am ↔ G. Log both
- [ ] Learn **Dm** (`xx0231`): finger 1 on string 1 fret 1, finger 2 on string 3 fret 2, finger 3 on string 2 fret 3
  - A compact triangle shape. Keep finger 3 arched so it doesn't mute string 1
  - Strum **only strings 4–1**. Aim your strum to start at string 4
- [ ] Pluck-check ×5, lift-and-land ×20
- [ ] Strum Dm, 4 downs ×10
- [ ] Slow changes: **Em → Dm** ×10 and **Dm → Am** ×10. These are the song's Line 4 → Line 5 changes

**Voice (5 min)**
- [ ] **Talk & strum (Drill A in [04 — Singing While Playing §4](04-Singing-While-Playing.md#4-drills-for-independence)):** strum Am, 4 downs at 65 BPM, and **count to 20 out loud**. Then say the alphabet. Then describe your day. The strum must not stop

**Record & Review (2 min)**
- [ ] Record Dm: pluck-check + strum

**Done when:** you can form Dm and play 4 downs on it.

## Day 11 — Four-chord day
**Date:** Mon Oct 5 · 🌙 post-shift: 30-minute cap

**Time:** ~35 min

**Warm-up (10 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body + Breathing
- [ ] Voice Warm-ups A + B

**Guitar (15 min)**
- [ ] Chord Check: all 4 chords (Em, Am, G, Dm)
- [ ] One-Minute Changes: **Em ↔ Dm** and **Dm ↔ Am** (new pairs). Log them
- [ ] **Chord roulette:** call out random chord names (or write them on cards and shuffle them) and play each one, 2 strums. 3 minutes
- [ ] Metronome 60, 4 downs per chord: **Am → Em → G → Am** (the song's main loop!), ×8 loops

**Voice (5 min)**
- [ ] **Sing along with the record**, twice. First time: just listen and hum. Second time: sing the words quietly. Hand over one ear
- [ ] Notice where the melody feels high, low, or easy. Note any problem spots in your log

**Together (3 min)**
- [ ] **Count aloud with the song loop:** Am → Em → G → Am, 4 downs each, counting "1 2 3 4" out loud. The count and the strum must stay locked

**Record & Review (2 min)**
- [ ] Record the Am → Em → G → Am loop

**Done when:** you can play the 4-chord loop in time, even with slightly late changes.

## Day 12 — The rhythm of the song
**Date:** Tue Oct 6 · 🟢 off day: full session

**Time:** ~35 min · **Reading:** [05 — Song Guide §2–4](05-Song-Guide-Aint-No-Sunshine.md)

**Warm-up (10 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body + Breathing (log your hiss)
- [ ] Voice Warm-ups A + B

**Guitar (15 min)**
- [ ] One-Minute Changes: your 2 **weakest** pairs. Log them
- [ ] **Split bars:** the song's turnaround is `| Em . G . |` (2 beats each). Metronome 60. Play:
  ```
  | Am . . . | Em . G . |
    ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓    ↓ ↓ ↓ ↓
  ```
  Say "Am-2-3-4, Em-2-G-4" out loud. ×10
- [ ] Play the Line 4 → 5 pattern: `| Em . . . | Dm . . . | Am . . . | Em . G . |` ×5
- [ ] **Read the whole chart** ([Song Guide §5](05-Song-Guide-Aint-No-Sunshine.md#5-the-full-song-on-one-page-performance-chart)) and play through Verse 1's chords once, as slowly as you need. **Don't worry about tempo.** Just find the chords in order

**Voice (5 min)**
- [ ] **Listening homework** ([Song Guide §10](05-Song-Guide-Aint-No-Sunshine.md#10-listening-homework-do-it-all-month)): listen once and count "1 2 3 4" along. Can you hear where the chord changes happen?
- [ ] Sing-along ×1, focusing on **Verse 1** only

**Together (3 min)**
- [ ] **Hum over the split bar:** strum `| Am . . . | Em . G . |` slowly and hum any comfortable note, one per bar

**Record & Review (2 min)**

**Done when:** you've played through Verse 1's chords, even slowly.

## Day 13 — Independence training
**Date:** Wed Oct 7 · 🟢 off day: full session

**Time:** ~35 min · **Reading:** [04 — Singing While Playing §3–5](04-Singing-While-Playing.md)

**Warm-up (10 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body + Breathing
- [ ] Voice Warm-ups A + B

**Guitar (10 min)**
- [ ] One-Minute Changes: weakest 2 pairs
- [ ] Verse chords at 60 BPM, quarter-note downs, 2 times through without stopping. If you miss a change, **keep strumming** and catch the next bar

**Together (12 min)**: the drills
- [ ] **Drill A: Talk & strum** on the Am-Em-G-Am loop: describe the room you're in while playing. 2 min
- [ ] **Drill C: Long note, moving hand:** 8 downstrums on Am while holding one sung "ah" on A (or your comfortable octave). Breathe, repeat. Then Em on E. 3 min
- [ ] **Drill D: Moving voice, still hand:** strum Am once, let it ring, sing the 5-note minor scale (A B C D E D C B A) over it. 3 min
- [ ] **Drill E: Change on the word:** play Am (4 downs), say "change" on beat 1 of every 2nd bar and switch to Em exactly then. 2 min

**Record & Review (3 min)**
- [ ] Record Drill C and Drill D. Listen: does your voice stay on pitch while your hand moves?

**Done when:** you've done all four drills, even imperfectly.

## Week 2 troubleshooting quick hits
| Problem | Quick fix |
|---|---|
| Full G won't ring | Use easy G this week; practice full G shape 3 min/day; wrist forward, thumb low |
| G → Am is painfully slow | It's the hardest change in the song. Give it a daily One-Minute session. Lift all fingers together, look at where finger 2 lands (string 4, fret 2) |
| Dm string 1 is dead | Arch finger 3; press finger 1 on its very tip |
| Strumming speeds up | Metronome, always. Count out loud |
| Strum stops when I talk | Normal at first. Talk slower. Use simpler words (count to 10). Keep going |
| Scales feel out of tune | Play each note on piano/guitar first, then sing it. Slower. Record and check |
| Voice tired after 15 min | Take breaks. Straw phonation to "reset." Drink water. It builds up over weeks |
