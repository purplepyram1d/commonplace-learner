# 09 — Glossary

Every term used in this course, alphabetically. **Category** tells you whether it's a guitar, voice, music, or practice term.

| Term | Category | Definition |
|---|---|---|
| **Action** | Guitar | The height of the strings above the fretboard. Low action = easier to play |
| **Anchor finger** | Guitar | A finger that stays in place (or on the same string) between two chords, making the change easier |
| **Anchor syllable** | Together | A sung syllable that lands exactly on a beat 1 or a chord change. Used to sync voice and guitar |
| **Appoggio** | Voice | Italian for "support": balanced, controlled breath support for singing |
| **Arpeggio** | Music | The notes of a chord played one after another instead of together |
| **Bar (measure)** | Music | A group of beats. In 4/4, one bar = 4 beats |
| **Bar line** | Music | The vertical line (a "pipe" character) separating bars in a chart |
| **Barre chord** | Guitar | A chord where one finger presses several strings at once. Not used in Month 1 |
| **Beat** | Music | The steady pulse of the music |
| **BPM** | Music | Beats per minute: the tempo |
| **Break (passaggio)** | Voice | The point where the voice shifts between chest and head registers, and sometimes cracks |
| **Bridge (guitar)** | Guitar | The wooden piece on the body where the strings are anchored |
| **Bridge (song)** | Music | A contrasting section of a song. In this song, the "I know" section |
| **Bridge pins** | Guitar | Pegs that hold the strings' ball ends in the bridge |
| **Callous** | Guitar | Hardened skin on the fingertips from fretting, which makes playing painless |
| **Capo** | Guitar | A clamp across the strings at a fret that raises the pitch of all the strings |
| **Chest voice** | Voice | The lower, fuller register that feels like it vibrates in the chest; close to your speaking voice |
| **Chord** | Music | Three or more notes played together |
| **Chord box / diagram** | Guitar | A picture of where to put your fingers for a chord |
| **Chromatic** | Music | Moving by half steps (one fret at a time) |
| **Count-in** | Practice | Counting "1, 2, 3, 4" before starting, to set the tempo |
| **Cool-down** | Voice | Gentle exercises after singing to return the voice to speaking mode |
| **Diaphragm** | Voice | The dome-shaped muscle under the lungs. It's the main breathing muscle |
| **Diphthong** | Voice | A vowel that glides between two sounds (like "i" in "time" = "ah-ee") |
| **Downbeat** | Music | Beat 1 of a bar, the strongest beat |
| **Downstrum** | Guitar | Strumming toward the floor, from the low strings to the high |
| **Dynamics** | Music | Changes in volume (soft, loud, building) |
| **Eighth notes** | Music | Two notes per beat, counted "1 & 2 & 3 & 4 &" |
| **Em7** | Guitar | E minor seventh: a softer version of Em. Here `020000` |
| **Falsetto** | Voice | A light, airy upper register |
| **Fingerboard / fretboard** | Guitar | The front face of the neck where you press the strings |
| **Flat (♭)** | Music | (1) A note lowered by a half step. (2) Singing or playing slightly too low |
| **Fret** | Guitar | (Noun) The metal strips on the fretboard. (Verb) To press a string down |
| **Fret code** | Guitar | A 6-character chord notation, low E to high e (e.g. Am = `x02210`) |
| **Ghost strum / miss** | Guitar | Moving the strumming hand past the strings without hitting them, to keep time |
| **Guide finger** | Guitar | A finger that slides along the same string between chords |
| **Half step (semitone)** | Music | The smallest distance between notes in Western music: one fret |
| **Head voice** | Voice | The higher, lighter register that resonates in the head |
| **Headstock** | Guitar | The top end of the guitar, where the tuners are |
| **Hiss ladder** | Voice | A breathing exercise: hiss "sss" steadily for as long as is comfortable, and time it |
| **Intro** | Music | The opening section before the vocals |
| **Key** | Music | The home note/chord of a song. "Ain't No Sunshine" is in A minor |
| **Larynx** | Voice | The voice box, which houses the vocal folds |
| **Layering Method** | Together | This course's step-by-step way to combine singing and playing (see file 04) |
| **Lip trill** | Voice | Blowing through loose lips to make a "brrr" motorboat sound while voicing. A key warm-up |
| **Major** | Music | A chord or key with a bright, happy sound |
| **Melody** | Music | The tune: a sequence of single notes, sung by the voice |
| **Metronome** | Practice | A device or app that clicks a steady beat |
| **Minor** | Music | A chord or key with a darker, sadder sound |
| **Minor pentatonic** | Music | A 5-note scale (A-C-D-E-G in A minor), the basis of blues and soul melodies |
| **Mix (voice)** | Voice | A blend of chest and head voice |
| **Muted string** | Guitar | A string that doesn't ring, either on purpose (x) or by accident (a finger touching it) |
| **Nut** | Guitar | The strip between the headstock and the fretboard |
| **Octave** | Music | The same note, higher or lower: 12 half steps apart |
| **Off-beat** | Music | The "&" between beats |
| **One-Minute Changes** | Practice | A drill: count how many times you can switch between two chords in 60 seconds |
| **Open chord** | Guitar | A chord that includes open (unfretted) strings. All 4 chords in this song are open chords |
| **Open string** | Guitar | A string played without pressing any fret |
| **Outro** | Music | The ending section of a song |
| **Palm mute** | Guitar | Resting the side of your strumming hand on the strings near the bridge, for a muffled, percussive sound |
| **Pick (plectrum)** | Guitar | A small plastic piece used to strum or pick |
| **Pickup (anacrusis)** | Music | Notes sung before beat 1 of the bar where the phrase "starts" |
| **Pitch** | Music | How high or low a note is |
| **Pluck** | Guitar | Playing a single string |
| **Pluck-check** | Practice | Playing each string of a chord one at a time to find buzzing or muted strings |
| **Progression** | Music | A sequence of chords |
| **Quarter notes** | Music | One note (or strum) per beat |
| **Register** | Voice | A range of the voice with a particular quality (chest, head, falsetto) |
| **Resonance** | Voice | How the sound rings and is amplified in the throat, mouth and face |
| **Rhythm** | Music | The pattern of sounds and silences in time |
| **Ritardando (rit.)** | Music | Gradually slowing down, usually at the end |
| **Root** | Music | The note a chord is named after (A in Am) |
| **Saddle** | Guitar | The strip in the bridge that the strings rest on |
| **Scale** | Music | A set of notes in order, e.g. do-re-mi-fa-so-la-ti-do |
| **Setup** | Guitar | A technician's adjustment of the action, neck and intonation to make a guitar play well |
| **Sharp (♯)** | Music | (1) A note raised by a half step. (2) Singing or playing slightly too high |
| **Siren** | Voice | A smooth slide from low to high and back, like a siren |
| **Soundhole** | Guitar | The round hole in the body |
| **SOVT** | Voice | Semi-Occluded Vocal Tract: exercises like lip trills, humming and straw phonation that make singing easier and safer |
| **Straw phonation** | Voice | Humming or singing through a straw. A very effective, safe warm-up |
| **Strum** | Guitar | Brushing the pick or fingers across several strings |
| **Strumming pattern** | Guitar | A repeated sequence of down- and up-strums |
| **Support** | Voice | Controlled, steady breath flow for singing |
| **Swing** | Music | A rhythm feel where off-beats are delayed: "daaa-da daaa-da" |
| **Tab (tablature)** | Guitar | Notation showing which string and fret to play, using numbers on six lines |
| **Tempo** | Music | The speed of the music, measured in BPM |
| **Time signature** | Music | How beats are grouped. 4/4 = four beats per bar |
| **Transpose** | Music | Move a song to a different key |
| **Triad** | Music | A 3-note chord: root, third, fifth |
| **Truss rod** | Guitar | A metal rod inside the neck that adjusts its curve. Leave it to a technician |
| **Tuner** | Guitar | A device or app that shows whether a string (or your voice) is in tune |
| **Tuning pegs / machine heads** | Guitar | The knobs used to tighten or loosen the strings |
| **Turnaround** | Music | A short chord movement that leads back to the start of a phrase. Here: `Em – G → Am` |
| **Upstrum** | Guitar | Strumming toward the ceiling, from the high strings to the low |
| **Verse** | Music | A section repeated with the same music but different words |
| **Vocal folds (cords)** | Voice | The two small bands of tissue in the larynx that vibrate to make sound |
| **Warm-up** | Practice | Exercises that prepare the body, hands or voice before practice |
| **Whole note** | Music | A note (or strum) lasting a full 4-beat bar |
