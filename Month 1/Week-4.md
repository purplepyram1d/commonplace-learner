# Week 4 — Putting It Together & Performing (Days 22–28)

> **Theme:** Go from "I can get through it" to "I can *play* it." Add the full strum, fix the weak spots, bring in dynamics and feel, and finish with a performance and a final recording.
>
> **The finish line:** On Day 28 you record a complete, unbroken take of "Ain't No Sunshine," playing and singing.

### Week 4 goals
By Day 28 you can:
- [ ] Play and sing the complete song with the **folk strum pattern** at **70–76 BPM**
- [ ] Keep going through mistakes without stopping
- [ ] Use **dynamics** (soft verses, building bridge, fuller Verse 3, soft outro)
- [ ] Start the song confidently: get your note, count in, intro, first line
- [ ] End the song cleanly: slow down and let the final Am ring
- [ ] Perform it for at least one person (live, on a video call, or posted)
- [ ] Have a **final recording** you're proud of (or at least not ashamed of!)

**Session template:** [Week 3–4 template](06-Daily-Routines.md#week-34-template-45-min) (~45–60 min)

**Your targets list:** from your Day 21 check-in, you have 3 weak guitar bars and 3 weak vocal lines. Every day this week starts with 5 minutes on them.

## Day 22 — Fix the weak spots, add the full strum
**Date:** Fri Oct 16 · 🌙 post-shift: 30-minute cap

**Time:** ~45 min

**Warm-up (12 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body + Breathing
- [ ] Voice Warm-ups A + C
- [ ] One-Minute Changes: weakest 2 pairs

**Targets (5 min)**
- [ ] **Loop** each weak guitar bar: play the bar before it + the weak bar + the bar after, 5× each, slowly
- [ ] **Loop** each weak vocal line with the record: listen, sing back, 3× each

**Guitar (8 min)**
- [ ] Folk pattern, full song, 65 BPM, no singing. **Goal: no switching to simpler strums.** ×1
- [ ] Metronome check: does it drift? Where?

**Together (15 min)**: Layering Step 8
- [ ] **Verse 1, singing, with the folk pattern**, 60 BPM. Very slow is fine. ×3
- [ ] If it breaks: sing that verse with quarter notes once, then go back to the pattern
- [ ] **Verse 2**, same way. ×3
- [ ] Note: the most common problem now is the **pattern bending to follow the voice**. Keep the hand pendulum going at all costs. Words can land between strums

**Record & Review (3 min)**
- [ ] Record Verse 1 with the full pattern

**Cool-down (2 min)**

**Done when:** you've sung Verse 1 over the folk pattern at least once without stopping.

## Day 23 — Tempo & the whole song with the pattern
**Date:** Sat Oct 17 · 🌙 post-shift: 30-minute cap

**Time:** ~45 min

**Warm-up (12 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body + Breathing
- [ ] Voice Warm-ups A + C
- [ ] Guitar Warm-up C at 70 BPM

**Targets (5 min)**
- [ ] Weak bars & lines (as Day 22)

**Together (23 min)**
- [ ] V1 + V2 with the folk pattern at **65 BPM**, ×2
- [ ] **Bridge with singing**: palm-muted or light quarter notes, building. ×2
- [ ] **Whole song**, singing + folk pattern (bridge in quarter notes), at 65 BPM. **Don't stop.**
- [ ] If that went OK: once more at **70 BPM**
- [ ] **Play along with the recording** (only if you're playing in the original key with no capo. If you use a capo, skip this, or try it just for the guitar part while the record plays). Does your tempo match the record's? Most people find they're slower. That's fine

**Record & Review (3 min)**
- [ ] Record the whole song at your best tempo

**Cool-down (2 min)**

**Done when:** you've done a full sung-and-played run-through with the pattern.

## Day 24 — Dynamics & feel
**Date:** Sun Oct 18 · 🌙 post-shift: 30-minute cap

**Time:** ~45 min · **Reading:** [Song Guide §6: dynamics & Level 4](05-Song-Guide-Aint-No-Sunshine.md#6-strumming-arrangement)

**Warm-up (12 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body + Breathing
- [ ] Voice Warm-ups A + C
- [ ] One-Minute Changes: all 6 pairs, 30 s each (quick check)

**Targets (5 min)**

**Feel (10 min)**: guitar only
- [ ] **Soft vs. full:** play Verse 1 very softly (strumming near the neck, light touch), then Verse 3 fuller. Can you control it?
- [ ] **Swing it:** play the folk pattern with the "&"s slightly late, lazy and laid-back. Listen to the record while you do it. ×3 loops
- [ ] **Tighten the turnaround:** listen to the record and hear how quick the Em–G sits before the next line. Try matching it. (Optional. The 2+2 version is perfectly musical)
- [ ] Try **thumb strumming** instead of the pick on Verse 1. Keep whichever you prefer
- [ ] **Bonus:** the turnaround bass walk ([Song Guide §7](05-Song-Guide-Aint-No-Sunshine.md#7-optional-the-turnaround-bass-walk-week-4-bonus))

**Together (12 min)**
- [ ] **Whole song with dynamics** (see the dynamics table in the Song Guide): soft intro & V1 → medium V2 → building bridge → full V3 → soft outro with a slow ending. ×2
- [ ] **Voice dynamics:** match the guitar. Intimate in V1, more intensity in V3, never shouting
- [ ] **Ending practice:** the outro only, ×5. Slow down (rit.) over the last 2 bars, end on one Am strum, let it ring **until it's silent**, then stop. Don't move until the sound is gone

**Record & Review (3 min)**
- [ ] Record the whole song. Listen specifically for: can you hear the difference between Verse 1 and Verse 3?

**Cool-down (2 min)**

**Done when:** your recording has audible dynamic changes.

## Day 25 — Performance skills
**Date:** Mon Oct 19 · 🌙 post-shift: 30-minute cap

**Time:** ~45 min

**Warm-up (12 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body + Breathing
- [ ] Voice Warm-ups A + C

**The Start Routine (5 min)**: a pro habit. Do it identically every time:
1. Check tuning.
2. Take a slow belly breath; relax your shoulders.
3. Pluck your starting note; hum it softly.
4. Count in (in your head or softly out loud): "1, 2, 3, 4."
5. Play the intro (2 bars of Am).
6. Breathe on beat 4 of the intro, and sing.

Practice **just the start**: Start Routine → Line 1 → stop. ×5

**Mistake recovery (10 min)**
- [ ] Play the whole song, and **on purpose** make mistakes: play a wrong chord, skip a strum, forget a word. Each time, **keep going.** Wrong chord → keep strumming, fix it by the next bar. Forgot a word → hum or sing "la" on the melody and rejoin
- [ ] Remember: **the audience doesn't know the arrangement.** If you don't react, most mistakes go unnoticed. A stop or a grimace is the thing people notice

**Eyes up & presence (8 min)**
- [ ] Whole song **looking at a spot on the wall** (or at your phone camera) as much as possible
- [ ] Whole song imagining a friend sitting in front of you. **Sing to them**, not to the fretboard

**Optional: standing up.** With a strap, at sitting height. Chords feel different standing. Only try it if you want to perform standing.

**Record & Review (5 min)**
- [ ] Record the whole song. Rate it (1–10) on: guitar steadiness, vocal pitch, timing, feeling. Log your scores

**Cool-down (2 min)**

**Done when:** you've practiced the Start Routine and done one run-through with deliberate mistake recovery.

## Day 26 — Dress rehearsal
**Date:** Tue Oct 20 · 🟢 off day: full session

**Time:** ~45–60 min

**Warm-up (15 min)**
- [ ] Full warm-up: Setup, Body, Breathing, Voice A + C, a guitar warm-up

**Run-throughs (25 min)**
- [ ] **Take 1:** a full run as if performing: Start Routine, whole song, no stopping, final Am rings out. Record it
- [ ] Listen back. Fix **one thing** (just one) by looping that spot 5 times
- [ ] **Take 2:** again, full, recorded
- [ ] Rest your voice 5 minutes (guitar-only noodling, water)
- [ ] **Take 3:** full, recorded
- [ ] Pick your best take of the three. Save it as "Day 26 — best"

**Plan your performance (5 min)**
- [ ] **Who** will you play for tomorrow? (Partner, roommate, family, a friend on video, a voice memo sent to someone, or a post online)
- [ ] **When?** Set a time. Tell them
- [ ] What will you say before playing? Keep it simple: "I've been learning guitar and singing for a month. Here's 'Ain't No Sunshine.'"

**Cool-down (2 min)**

## Day 27 — Performance day 🎤
**Date:** Wed Oct 21 · 🟢 off day: full session

**Time:** ~30 min practice + your performance

**Before the performance**
- [ ] Short warm-up: Body, Breathing, Voice A, Chord Check. **Not too much practice.** Save your voice and energy
- [ ] One full run-through, then stop. Don't cram

**Nerves (they're normal)**
- A racing heart and shaky hands are adrenaline. It's your body getting ready, not a sign of failure
- **Breathing reset:** 4 slow belly breaths with a long exhale (4 in, 8 out) right before you play. It genuinely calms your nervous system
- Expect to play **a bit faster** than in practice (adrenaline). Consciously start slower
- Expect a mistake or two. You've practiced recovering: **keep going**
- Remember why you're doing it: you learned a song in 4 weeks. That's worth sharing

**The performance**
- [ ] Start Routine → play → let the last chord ring → smile. (Don't apologize before or after! "Sorry, I'm still learning…" makes listeners uncomfortable. Just play, then say thank you.)
- [ ] Record it if possible

**After**
- [ ] Write down in your log: how did it feel? What went better than expected? What went wrong, and did anyone notice?

**Date:** Tue Oct 27 · 🟢 off day: full session

**Compare**

**Month 1 reflection** (in your log):
1. What am I most proud of?
2. What was the hardest part of the month?
3. Which practice methods helped most?

5. Did I practice consistently? What got in the way?
6. What song do I want to learn next?

## What's next: a Month 2 preview
You now have a foundation that unlocks hundreds of songs. Natural next steps:

**Guitar**
- New chords: **C, D, E, A, Cadd9, E major**. With these plus your 4, you can play a huge number of pop, folk and rock songs
- Full G (`320033` variant) if you haven't already
- More strumming patterns and a real understanding of **swing and syncopation**
- Simple **fingerpicking** (thumb on bass, fingers on treble strings)
- The **A minor pentatonic scale** as a lead-guitar and fill tool (you already sang it!)
- Your first **barre chord** (F), in Months 2–3

**Voice**
- Extend your range gently (sirens and scales daily)
- Work on the **break** (chest ↔ head voice) with lip trills and sirens
- Vowel shaping and tone color
- Pitch accuracy with harder melodies (bigger jumps)
- Simple **harmony singing**

**Together**
- A second song with a **different strum**, and one with **faster chord changes**
- Songs in different keys, using the capo
- Learning songs **by ear**: you've already started (counting bars, finding your starting note)

**Songs that use similar skills** (check each for key and range):
- "Horse with No Name" (America): 2 chords, and a good strumming workout
- "Knockin' on Heaven's Door" (Bob Dylan): G, D, Am, C
- "Bad Moon Rising" (CCR): D, A, G
- "Wonderwall" (Oasis): Em7, G, Dsus4, A7sus4
- "Riptide" (Vance Joy): Am, G, C (a classic "next song")
- "Stand by Me" (Ben E. King): A, F♯m, D, E (use a capo for easier shapes)
- "Lean on Me" (Bill Withers): another Withers song, with simple chords
- "Zombie" (The Cranberries): Em, C, G, D

**Congratulations.** You started with nothing, and in 28 days you learned an instrument, trained your voice, and put them together into a real song. Most people who say "I've always wanted to learn guitar" never make it through Week 1. You did the whole month.
