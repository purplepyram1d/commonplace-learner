# Week 3 — Learning the Song (Days 15–21)

> **Theme:** Learn "Ain't No Sunshine" in three separate layers (the guitar part, the vocal part, and how they fit together), then start stacking them using the [Layering Method](04-Singing-While-Playing.md#2-the-layering-method-the-core-technique).
>
> **This is the week of "the wall."** At some point, singing and playing together will feel impossible. That's expected. Drop back a layer, go slower, and trust the method.

### Week 3 goals
By Day 21 you can:
- [ ] Play the **complete song's chords** from start to end using the performance chart, at **65+ BPM**, with quarter-note downs, without stopping
- [ ] Play the **folk strum pattern** (D - D U - U D U) on single chords, and on the verse pattern at 60 BPM
- [ ] Sing the **complete melody** along with the recording
- [ ] **Hum** the melody while strumming the progression
- [ ] **Speak** Verse 1's lyrics in rhythm while strumming
- [ ] **Sing** Verses 1 and 2 over whole-note strums (one strum per chord)
- [ ] Get through the "I know" section with planned breaths
- [ ] One-Minute Changes: **G ↔ Am 30+** and **Em ↔ Dm 25+**

**Session template:** [Week 3–4 template](06-Daily-Routines.md#week-34-template-45-min) (~45 min)

**Before Day 15:** print the lyrics from a licensed source with wide spacing, and print the [performance chart](05-Song-Guide-Aint-No-Sunshine.md#5-the-full-song-on-one-page-performance-chart). Pencil in the chords above the lyrics as you learn them this week.

## Day 15 — The map of the song
**Time:** ~45 min · **Reading:** [05 — Song Guide](05-Song-Guide-Aint-No-Sunshine.md), the whole thing

**Warm-up (12 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body + Breathing
- [ ] Voice Warm-ups A + C (C is new: pentatonic scale and starting note)
- [ ] One-Minute Changes: G ↔ Am, Em ↔ Dm

**Guitar (15 min)**
- [ ] Metronome **60 BPM**, quarter-note downs (Level 2)
- [ ] **Intro + Verse 1** from the chart, 3 times through
- [ ] **Loop the verse** (5 lines) 3 times without stopping. Missed a change? Keep strumming, catch the next bar

**Voice (12 min)**
- [ ] **Listen** to the song with the lyric sheet in hand, following the words with your finger
- [ ] **Pencil in** the chords above the words of Verse 1: where does Am start? Where does the Em–G turnaround happen? Where's the Dm?
- [ ] **Find your key** ([Song Guide §8](05-Song-Guide-Aint-No-Sunshine.md#finding-your-key)): sing Verse 1 along with the record. Too low? Try the octave, or test capo positions 3–5 while singing the title line over Am. **Decide your key today** and write it on your chart
- [ ] **Find your starting note** ([Song Guide §8](05-Song-Guide-Aint-No-Sunshine.md#finding-your-starting-note)) and write it down

**Record & Review (3 min)**
- [ ] Record Verse 1's chords at 60 BPM

**Done when:** you know your key, your starting note, and you can loop the verse chords.

## Day 16 — Whole-song chords & humming the melody
**Time:** ~45 min

**Warm-up (12 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body + Breathing
- [ ] Voice Warm-ups A + C
- [ ] One-Minute Changes: weakest 2 pairs

**Guitar (15 min)**
- [ ] Play the **whole song's chords** start to finish at 60 BPM, quarter-note downs: Intro → V1 → V2 → Bridge → V3 → Outro
- [ ] **Bridge:** count the Am bars with the record and write your count on the chart
- [ ] Do the whole run **twice**. Circle any spot on the chart where you stumble

**Voice (8 min)**
- [ ] Sing along with the record **on "la"** (no words), focusing on getting the tune right. ×2
- [ ] **Melody echo** (Routine 6): play 1 line of the record, pause, sing it back on "la." Verse 1, all 5 lines

**Together (7 min)**: Layering Step 4
- [ ] Strum **Level 1 (whole notes)** through Verse 1 and **hum** the melody over it
- [ ] Then **Level 2 (quarter notes)**, humming ×2
- [ ] If humming makes you lose the chords: go back to whole notes. That's the method working, not failing

**Record & Review (3 min)**
- [ ] Record: hummed Verse 1 over quarter-note strums

**Done when:** you've played the whole song's chords twice and hummed over Verse 1.

## Day 17 — The folk strum & speaking the words
**Time:** ~45 min · **Reading:** [01 — Guitar Fundamentals §11 (Pattern 4)](01-Guitar-Fundamentals.md), [Song Guide §6](05-Song-Guide-Aint-No-Sunshine.md#6-strumming-arrangement)

**Warm-up (12 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body + Breathing
- [ ] Voice Warm-ups A + C
- [ ] Guitar Warm-up C: "Strum Tune-up" (quarters, then eighths)

**Guitar (15 min)**
- [ ] **Learn the folk pattern:** `D - D U - U D U` ("DOWN, down-up, up-down-up")
  - Say it out loud while doing it, on **one chord** (Am), at **55 BPM**. Keep the hand moving down-up the whole time. The pattern comes from *missing* strings on some motions
  - 3 minutes on Am, then Em, then G, then Dm
- [ ] **Half pattern** for split bars: `D - D U` (beats 1–2) → change → `D - D U` (beats 3–4). Practice on `| Em . G . |` slowly
- [ ] Play `| Am . . . | Em . G . |` with the folk pattern ×10. **Use the open-string cheat** on the last up-strum before each change

**Voice (8 min)**
- [ ] **Speak the lyrics in rhythm** along with the record (no singing, just rhythmic speech, like rapping it). Verse 1 and 2. ×2
- [ ] Mark the **anchor syllables** ([04 §3](04-Singing-While-Playing.md#3-the-micro-level-syllable-strum-mapping)) on your lyric sheet: underline the word landing on each chord change

**Together (7 min)**: Layering Step 5
- [ ] Strum Verse 1 with **whole notes**, and **speak** the lyrics in rhythm over it
- [ ] Then quarter-note downs, speaking ×2
- [ ] Notice the **pickups**: which lines start before the Am? Mark them with an arrow **→** on your sheet

**Record & Review (3 min)**

**Done when:** you can do the folk pattern on single chords, and you've spoken Verse 1 over the chords.

## Day 18 — First real singing & playing
**Time:** ~45 min · **Reading:** [04 — Singing While Playing](04-Singing-While-Playing.md) (reread §2 and §5)

**Warm-up (12 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body + Breathing
- [ ] Voice Warm-ups A + C
- [ ] One-Minute Changes: weakest 2 pairs

**Guitar (10 min)**
- [ ] Folk pattern on the full verse at **55–60 BPM**, 3 times. Use quarter-note downs for any bar that falls apart
- [ ] Whole song, quarter notes, **65 BPM**, once

**Voice (8 min)**
- [ ] **Breath plan:** listen, and mark **✓** on your lyric sheet at every breath point (the turnarounds) for Verses 1–3
- [ ] Sing Verse 1 along with the record, with **words**, breathing at your ✓ marks. ×2

**Together (12 min)**: Layering Step 6 (**the big one**)
- [ ] **Get your note:** pluck your starting-note string during the 2 intro bars and hum it
- [ ] Count in: "1, 2, 3, 4"
- [ ] **Sing Verse 1 over whole-note strums** (one strum per chord). Your voice leads; the strum just marks the changes
- [ ] Repeat ×5. If it falls apart, go line by line: Line 1 ×3, Line 2 ×3, then Lines 1–2 together, etc.
- [ ] **Pickup practice:** end of Line 1 → pickup → start of Line 2, ×5

**Record & Review (3 min)**
- [ ] **Record your first sung and played verse.** It will be rough. Save it! It's a milestone

**Done when:** you've sung Verse 1 while playing whole-note strums, all the way through at least once.

## Day 19 — The "I know" section
**Time:** ~45 min · **Reading:** [Song Guide §8: the "I know" mini-lesson](05-Song-Guide-Aint-No-Sunshine.md#the-i-know-section-a-mini-lesson)

**Warm-up (12 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body + Breathing (log your hiss; aim for 25+ s)
- [ ] Voice Warm-ups A + C
- [ ] Guitar Warm-up C

**Guitar (8 min)**
- [ ] Bridge on its own: Am, quarter-note downs, for your counted number of bars, then the 2 following lines. ×5
- [ ] Try the **palm mute** on the bridge ([01 §11](01-Guitar-Fundamentals.md#palm-muting-optional-week-4)), optional

**"I know" mini-lesson (15 min)**: follow the steps in the Song Guide
- [ ] Step 1: **speak** the "I know"s in rhythm with the record, counting reps
- [ ] Step 2: speak them **while strumming** Am
- [ ] Step 3: **sing** them lightly while strumming
- [ ] Step 4: add **planned breaths** (mark them on your sheet: e.g. every 4–6 reps)
- [ ] Step 5: add the **build** (quiet → louder)

**Together (7 min)**
- [ ] Verse 1 sung over whole notes ×2
- [ ] **Verse 2** sung over whole notes ×3
- [ ] Try **V1 → V2** back to back

**Record & Review (3 min)**
- [ ] Record the "I know" section with guitar

**Done when:** you get through the "I know" section with guitar without running out of breath.

## Day 20 — Quarter notes under the voice
**Time:** ~45 min

**Warm-up (12 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body + Breathing
- [ ] Voice Warm-ups A + C
- [ ] One-Minute Changes: G ↔ Am, Em ↔ Dm (log them against the Week 3 targets)

**Guitar (8 min)**
- [ ] Folk pattern, full verse, **60–65 BPM**, ×3
- [ ] Whole song with the folk pattern, once. Quarter-note downs are fine for the tricky bars

**Together (20 min)**: Layering Step 7
- [ ] **Whole song, singing, over whole notes**: Intro → V1 → V2 → Bridge → V3 → Outro. Don't stop for mistakes
- [ ] Then **Verse 1 with quarter-note downs** (4 per bar) while singing ×3
- [ ] Then **Verse 2** the same way ×3
- [ ] **Drill F: Stop & go** ([04 §4](04-Singing-While-Playing.md#4-drills-for-independence)): sing Verse 1 and stop strumming mid-line (keep singing), then rejoin on the next beat 1. ×3
- [ ] **Eyes-up check** (Drill G): sing one verse looking at the wall, not your hands

**Record & Review (3 min)**
- [ ] Record the whole song with whole notes

**Cool-down (2 min)**

**Done when:** you've sung the whole song through (whole-note strums) without stopping.

## Day 21 — Check-in & rest
**Time:** ~20 min (light)

**Week 3 check-in:**
- [ ] Tune + short warm-up
- [ ] One-Minute Changes on all 6 pairs. Log them and compare with Weeks 1 and 2
- [ ] **Record a full take:** the whole song, singing, with whatever strum level works (whole notes or quarter notes). **No stopping**
- [ ] Watch it back. Write down:
  - The 3 bars where the guitar goes wrong most
  - The 3 lines where the singing is weakest (pitch, timing, or breath)
  - These are your **Week 4 targets**
- [ ] Tick off the Week 3 goals

**Rest the voice for the rest of the day.** Do mental practice ([04 §6](04-Singing-While-Playing.md#6-mental-practice-its-real-practice)): run through the song in your head, feeling the chord changes.

## Week 3 troubleshooting quick hits
| Problem | Quick fix |
|---|---|
| Strumming stops the moment I sing | Back to whole notes. Then hum, then speak. [Layering Method](04-Singing-While-Playing.md#2-the-layering-method-the-core-technique) |
| Singing goes where the strumming goes (can't separate them) | Drills B and D. Speak lyrics over strumming a lot |
| I come in late on each line | Pickups! Count out loud; start on "4" or "&" of the bar before. Practice the transitions only |
| I run out of air | Breathe at every turnaround; don't start lines too loud; practice the hiss |
| Folk pattern falls apart when changing chords | Use quarter-note downs for the song this week; practice the pattern separately. By Week 4 it'll merge |
| I lose my place in the "I know" section | Count bars, not "I know"s; mark the chart |
| My voice is tired | Split practice: guitar-only block, then voice block. Hum instead of singing on some runs. Cool down |
| I'm frustrated | Normal! Look at your Day 1 video. Take a day off if needed |
