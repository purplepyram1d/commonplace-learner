# 06 — Daily Routines

These are the routines you'll repeat every day. The weekly plans refer to them by name ("Do **Body Warm-up** + **Voice Warm-up A**"). Keep this page open while you practice.

---

## Routine 0: Setup (1–2 min, every session)
- [ ] Wash hands (keeps strings alive, and your fingers grip better)
- [ ] Water within reach
- [ ] Phone ready to record
- [ ] **Tune the guitar**, all 6 strings, then check them all again
- [ ] Posture check: front half of chair, feet flat, tall spine, shoulders down

---

## Routine 1: Body Warm-up (2 min)
Do it standing.
1. **Shoulder rolls**: 5 backward, 5 forward.
2. **Neck release**: gently tilt your ear toward your shoulder, hold 10 seconds each side. **No neck circles** (they strain the neck).
3. **Arm swings**: let your arms swing loosely side to side, twisting your torso, 10 times.
4. **Wrist circles**: 5 each direction, both wrists.
5. **Fist-open**: make a tight fist, then spread your fingers as wide as possible. 10 times.
6. **Finger taps**: touch each fingertip to your thumb (1-2-3-4-3-2-1), 3 times each hand.
7. **Gentle forearm stretch**: arm straight out, palm up, gently pull your fingers back with the other hand. 15 seconds. Then palm down, gently press the back of your hand. 15 seconds. **Gentle**, never to the point of pain.
8. **Shake out** your hands like you're flicking off water.

---

## Routine 2: Breathing (2–3 min)
1. **Belly check** (30 s): one hand on your belly, one on your chest. 3 slow breaths, belly hand moving only.
2. **4-4-8** (1 min): inhale 4 counts, hold 4 gently, exhale 8 on "sss." ×4.
3. **Hiss ladder** (30–60 s): big low breath, hiss "sssss" evenly for as long as is comfortable (don't squeeze out the last drop). **Time it and log it.**
4. **Staccato "ts"** (30 s): "ts-ts-ts-ts" ×8, 4 sets. Feel your belly bounce inward.

---

## Routine 3: Voice Warm-ups

### Voice Warm-up A: "Gentle" (5 min), Weeks 1–4 (every day)
| # | Exercise | Duration |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Yawn-sigh**: start a yawn, then sigh out from high to low on "haaaah" | ×3 |
| 2 | **Humming** at your comfortable speaking pitch, feeling the buzz on your lips | 30 s |
| 3 | **Lip trills**: steady, then slide up and down a little like a gentle "hill" | 1 min |
| 4 | **Straw phonation** (or "ng" if no straw): slide low → high → low slowly, like a siren | 1 min |
| 5 | **Lip-trill sirens**: bigger slides, bottom to top of your comfortable range | 30 s |
| 6 | **"Mmm-ah"**: hum "mmm," then open to "ah" on the same pitch, keeping the buzz. Various comfortable pitches | 1 min |

### Voice Warm-up B: "Scales" (5 min), Week 2 onward (add after A)
Use a piano app (e.g. "Perfect Piano," "Piano"), a keyboard, or search for "5 note scale vocal warm up" to follow along.

1. **Five-note major scale** (do-re-mi-fa-so-fa-mi-re-do), on **"mah"**:
   - Start at a low, comfortable note.
   - Move up a half step each repetition, 5–8 times.
   - Then come back down.
2. **Five-note minor scale** (e.g. A-B-C-D-E-D-C-B-A), on **"noo"** or **"mee"**.
3. **Arpeggio** (1-3-5-3-1, i.e. do-mi-so-mi-do) on **"yah"**.

### Voice Warm-up C: "Song Prep" (3 min), Week 3 onward
1. **A minor pentatonic scale**: A-C-D-E-G-A up and down on "ah" (sing it an octave up if needed). Play each note on the guitar first if you're unsure. On the A string: open (A), fret 3 (C), fret 5 (D), fret 7 (E), fret 10 (G), fret 12 (A).
2. **Starting-note check**: pluck your song's starting note (see [Song Guide §8](05-Song-Guide-Aint-No-Sunshine.md#finding-your-starting-note)), hum it, then sing it on "ah."
3. **Speak-sing the title line**: say it on one pitch, then sing it with the melody, lightly.

### Cool-down (1–2 min), after sessions with 20+ minutes of singing
- Gentle descending lip-trill or straw slides, high to low, ×5
- Humming at your speaking pitch, 30 s
- Speak a sentence normally. Your voice should feel easy, not tired

## Routine 4: Guitar Warm-ups

### Guitar Warm-up A: "Spider" (3 min), Week 1 onward
The **1-2-3-4 chromatic exercise** builds finger strength, independence and accuracy.
On each string, starting with the low E (string 6), play frets 1, 2, 3, 4 in order, then move to the next string and repeat, ending on the high e (string 1).
- One finger per fret: finger 1 on fret 1, 2 on 2, 3 on 3, 4 on 4.
- Pluck each note once, with a downstroke of the pick.
- Go slowly. Each note should ring clearly. Press right behind the fret.
- Go up (string 6 → 1), then back down (string 1 → 6, playing 4-3-2-1 on each string).
- **Week 1:** as slow as needed. **Week 4:** with a metronome at 60 BPM, one note per click, then two per click.
- Pinky weak? Totally normal. It's the finger that improves the most from this exercise.

### Guitar Warm-up B: "Chord Check" (1–2 min), Week 1 onward
For each chord you know:
1. Form the chord.
2. **Pluck each string one at a time**, from the lowest string that belongs in the chord to string 1.
3. Every note should ring clearly. Fix any buzzing or muted string.
4. Strum it once and let it ring.

### Guitar Warm-up C: "Strum Tune-up" (2 min), Week 2 onward
Metronome at the current tempo:
1. 8 bars of **quarter-note downs** on Am (with the hand moving up between beats, missing the strings)
2. 8 bars of **eighth notes** (D U D U D U D U)
3. 8 bars of the **folk pattern** (D - D U - U D U), from Week 3

## Routine 5: One-Minute Changes (3–6 min), Day 3 onward
See [01 — Guitar Fundamentals §10](01-Guitar-Fundamentals.md#the-one-minute-changes-drill-do-it-daily-from-day-3).

**Pairs, in order of introduction:**
| Pair | Introduced | Song relevance |
|---|---|---|
| Em ↔ Am | Day 3 | ★★★ constant |
| Am ↔ G | Day 9 | ★★★ constant (G → Am) |
| Em ↔ G | Day 9 | ★★★ constant |
| Em ↔ Dm | Day 11 | ★★ once per verse |
| Dm ↔ Am | Day 11 | ★★ once per verse |
| Am ↔ Dm | Day 12 | ★ general skill |

Do **2–3 pairs** per day. Prioritize your **lowest** scores. Log every score.

---

## Routine 6: Ear Training (2–3 min)
Pick one per day:
- **Match the string**: pluck a string, hum, check with a tuner app
- **Up or down?**: use an ear-training app (e.g. "Functional Ear Trainer," "Perfect Ear," "Tenuto," or musictheory.net's free exercises)
- **Echo**: play 3 notes on one string, then sing them back
- **Major or minor?**: strum Am, then G, or have an app play chords, and say "sad" or "happy"
- **Melody echo** (Week 3+): play 2–4 seconds of the song, pause, sing it back

---

## Routine 7: Record & Review (3–5 min), every session from Day 1
1. **Record** one thing from today's session (a chord change, a scale, a verse, whatever the day's focus was). Video is best.
2. **Listen or watch back immediately**, once.
3. Write in your log:
   - **One thing that was good**
   - **One thing to fix tomorrow**
4. Save the recording in a "Month 1" album or folder. You'll compare them on Day 28.

## The standard session templates

### Week 1 template (~30 min)
| Min | Block |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | Routine 0: Setup |
| 2–4 | Routine 1: Body Warm-up |
| 4–7 | Routine 2: Breathing |
| 7–12 | Voice Warm-up A |
| 12–25 | **Day's main lesson** (guitar + voice) |
| 25–28 | Routine 6: Ear Training |
| 28–30 | Routine 7: Record & Review |

### Week 2 template (~35 min)
| Min | Block |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | Setup |
| 2–4 | Body Warm-up |
| 4–6 | Breathing |
| 6–11 | Voice Warm-ups A + B (shortened) |
| 11–14 | Guitar Warm-up A (Spider) |
| 14–19 | One-Minute Changes (2–3 pairs) |
| 19–31 | **Day's main lesson** |
| 31–35 | Record & Review |

### Week 3–4 template (~45 min)
| Min | Block |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | Setup |
| 2–4 | Body Warm-up + Breathing (short) |
| 4–10 | Voice Warm-ups A + C |
| 10–13 | Guitar Warm-ups A/B/C (pick 1–2) |
| 13–17 | One-Minute Changes (weakest 2 pairs) |
| 17–40 | **Day's main lesson** (song work) |
| 40–43 | Record & Review |
| 43–45 | Cool-down |

### Short-day template (15 min, for busy days: better than skipping)
| Min | Block |
|---|---|
| 0–2 | Tune + posture |
| 2–5 | Lip trills + straw |
| 5–8 | One-Minute Changes (1 pair) |
| 8–14 | Play or sing the current focus once or twice |
| 14–15 | Log it |
