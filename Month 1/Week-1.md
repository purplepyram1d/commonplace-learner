# Week 1 — Foundations (Days 1–7)

> **Theme:** Get comfortable with the instrument and your voice. Build the habits (tuning, posture, breathing, warming up) that everything else rests on.
>
> **Chords this week:** Em, Am (and Em7)
> **Voice this week:** breathing, posture, gentle warm-ups, matching a single pitch
> **Together this week:** humming and counting over a single strummed chord

### Week 1 goals
By Day 7 you can:
- [ ] Tune the guitar with a tuner, unaided, in under 2 minutes
- [ ] Sit and hold the guitar correctly, with the guitar staying put when your left hand lets go
- [ ] Play **Em** and **Am** cleanly (every string in the chord rings)
- [ ] Change Em ↔ Am at **15+ times per minute**
- [ ] Strum 4 steady downstrums per bar at 60 BPM
- [ ] Belly-breathe without lifting your shoulders
- [ ] Hiss steadily for **15+ seconds**
- [ ] Hum and match the pitch of an open guitar string (confirmed by a tuner app)
- [ ] Hum a steady note while strumming a chord

### Before Day 1
Read [00 — Start Here](00-Start-Here-Gear-and-Setup.md) and do its "Before your very first session" checklist, including the **Day 0 video**.

## Day 1 — Meet your instruments
**Time:** ~30 min · **Reading:** [01 — Guitar Fundamentals §1–4](01-Guitar-Fundamentals.md), [02 — Voice Fundamentals §1–3](02-Voice-Fundamentals.md)

**Warm-up (5 min)**
- [ ] Routine 1: Body Warm-up ([06 — Daily Routines](06-Daily-Routines.md))
- [ ] Belly breathing on the floor with a book on your belly, 10 breaths

**Guitar (15 min)**
- [ ] Name every part of the guitar out loud (headstock, tuners, nut, neck, frets, body, soundhole, bridge, saddle)
- [ ] Learn the string names: **E A D G B E** (Eddie Ate Dynamite, Good Bye Eddie). Pluck each string while saying its name, 3 times
- [ ] **Tune the guitar** with your tuner. Take your time. Then detune one string on purpose (a quarter turn) and retune it. Repeat with 3 different strings
- [ ] Set up your **sitting posture** (Guitar Fundamentals §4). Do the "let go with your left hand" test
- [ ] Hold the pick correctly. Strum all 6 open strings slowly with downstrums, 20 times. Listen to the sound: that's an **Em11-ish open-string chord**, and it should sound pleasant if you're in tune

**Voice (8 min)**
- [ ] Standing posture reset ([Voice Fundamentals §2](02-Voice-Fundamentals.md#2-posture-for-singing))
- [ ] Belly breathing standing, hand on belly and chest, 10 breaths
- [ ] **Hiss test:** big breath, "sssss" as long as is comfortable. Time it. Write it down (this is your baseline)
- [ ] Humming: hum at a comfortable pitch for 5 breaths. Find the buzz on your lips

**Together (0 min)**: not yet!

**Record & Review (2 min)**
- [ ] Record yourself saying the string names and strumming the open strings

**Done when:** your guitar is tuned, you know the string names, and you've timed your first hiss.

## Day 2 — Your first chord: Em
**Time:** ~30 min · **Reading:** [01 — Guitar Fundamentals §5, 7, 9 (Em)](01-Guitar-Fundamentals.md)

**Warm-up (8 min)**
- [ ] Setup (tune!) + Body Warm-up
- [ ] Routine 2: Breathing (the 4-4-8 and hiss ladder)

**Guitar (15 min)**
- [ ] Learn **finger numbers** (1 index, 2 middle, 3 ring, 4 pinky)
- [ ] **Fretting test:** put finger 1 on string 6 (low E), fret 1, right behind the fret. Pluck it. Buzzing? Move closer to the fret, press more. Now release pressure until it buzzes, then add back just enough. Repeat on a few strings. That's your "minimum pressure"
- [ ] Form **Em** (`022000`): finger 2 on string 5 fret 2, finger 3 on string 4 fret 2
- [ ] **Pluck each string** 6 → 1, one at a time. All should ring. Fix any dead strings (arch fingers, tips only)
- [ ] Strum Em: 4 slow downstrums, stop, check, repeat. ×10
- [ ] **Lift and land:** lift your fingers ~1 cm off the strings, keep the shape, land again. ×20. (It trains the chord shape into your hand)
- [ ] Try **Em7** (`020000`, just finger 2). Hear the softer sound

**Voice (5 min)**
- [ ] Voice Warm-up A (yawn-sigh, hum, lip trills, straw/"ng")
- [ ] If lip trills don't work: fingers on the cheeks, more air, looser lips. It often takes a few days. Use straw or humming meanwhile

**Record & Review (2 min)**
- [ ] Record Em: pluck each string, then strum it 4 times

**Done when:** Em rings with all 6 strings clear, at least most of the time.

## Day 3 — Second chord: Am, and your first chord change
**Time:** ~30 min · **Reading:** [01 — Guitar Fundamentals §9 (Am), §10](01-Guitar-Fundamentals.md)

**Warm-up (10 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body Warm-up + Breathing
- [ ] Voice Warm-up A

**Guitar (15 min)**
- [ ] Review Em: form it, pluck-check it, strum it ×5
- [ ] Learn **Am** (`x02210`): finger 1 on string 2 fret 1, finger 2 on string 4 fret 2, finger 3 on string 3 fret 2
- [ ] Notice: **Em → Am = fingers 2 and 3 move down one string each, then add finger 1.** Same shape!
- [ ] Pluck-check Am (strings 5 → 1). The most common problem is finger 1 touching string 1. Arch it
- [ ] Strum Am from string 5, 4 downstrums ×10
- [ ] **Slow changes:** Em (4 strums) → Am (4 strums) → Em … ×10. Take as long as you need between them
- [ ] **Your first One-Minute Change: Em ↔ Am.** Timer on 60 s, 1 strum per chord, count each change. **Write down the number.** (Anything from 5 to 20 is normal today)

**Voice (3 min)**
- [ ] **Pitch match #1:** pluck the open **A string** (5). Listen. Hum it. Slide until it "locks." Check with a tuner app on your phone (it should say A). Higher voices: hum the A an octave up
- [ ] Repeat with the **D string** (4)

**Record & Review (2 min)**
- [ ] Record your Em ↔ Am slow change

**Done when:** you've logged your first One-Minute Change score.

## Day 4 — Strumming in time
**Time:** ~30 min · **Reading:** [01 — Guitar Fundamentals §11–12](01-Guitar-Fundamentals.md), [03 — Music Basics §4](03-Music-Basics-You-Actually-Need.md)

**Warm-up (10 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body + Breathing (log your hiss time)
- [ ] Voice Warm-up A

**Guitar (13 min)**
- [ ] Guitar Warm-up A: "Spider" (1-2-3-4) on strings 6, 5, 4 only today, slowly
- [ ] One-Minute Changes: Em ↔ Am. Log the score
- [ ] **Metronome at 60 BPM.** Listen to 4 clicks and count "1, 2, 3, 4" out loud
- [ ] Strum **Em with one downstrum on every click**. Try to "bury" the click. 2 minutes
- [ ] **Keep the hand moving:** down on the click, up (missing the strings) between clicks. The hand never stops. 2 minutes
- [ ] Same on Am
- [ ] **4 + 4:** 4 downs on Em, 4 downs on Am, repeat. Changing **in time** is hard! If you're late, it's fine. Keep going and catch the next beat 1

**Voice (5 min)**
- [ ] **Find your range** ([Voice Fundamentals §6](02-Voice-Fundamentals.md#6-finding-your-range-and-voice-type)): with a tuner app, find your lowest and highest **comfortable** notes. Write them in your log
- [ ] Pitch match: A string and D string, then try the G string (3)

**Record & Review (2 min)**
- [ ] Record 30 seconds of Em/Am changes with the metronome

**Done when:** you can strum 4 downs per chord with the metronome, even if the changes are messy.

## Day 5 — First "together": humming over a chord
**Time:** ~30 min · **Reading:** [04 — Singing While Playing §1–2](04-Singing-While-Playing.md)

**Warm-up (10 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body + Breathing
- [ ] Voice Warm-up A

**Guitar (10 min)**
- [ ] Spider: all 6 strings, slowly
- [ ] Chord Check (Guitar Warm-up B): Em, Am
- [ ] One-Minute Changes: Em ↔ Am. Log it
- [ ] Metronome 60: 4 downs Em, 4 downs Am, ×8 cycles

**Together (8 min)**: your first coordination work
- [ ] **Count aloud while strumming:** Em, 4 downs, saying "ONE, two, three, four" with each strum. Then Am. 2 minutes. Keep going even if it feels silly
- [ ] **Strum once, hum:** strum Am once, let it ring, and hum the open **A string's** pitch (pluck it first). Hold the hum for 4 slow counts. ×5
- [ ] **Strum steadily, hum:** Am, 4 downs per bar at 60 BPM, and hum one long A note over it (breathe when you need to). **The strumming must not stop when you breathe.** 1 minute
- [ ] Same with Em, humming an E (pluck the open low E or high e for the pitch)

**Record & Review (2 min)**
- [ ] Record the "strum + hum" exercise

**Done when:** you can hum a steady note while strumming steady downs for 30 seconds.

## Day 6 — Consolidate & first check-in
**Time:** ~30 min

**Warm-up (10 min)**
- [ ] Setup + Body + Breathing (log your hiss)
- [ ] Voice Warm-up A

**Guitar (10 min)**
- [ ] Spider ×2
- [ ] One-Minute Changes: Em ↔ Am ×2 (log both, and compare with Day 3!)
- [ ] **Eyes-closed Em and Am:** form each chord with your eyes closed, open them and check. ×10
- [ ] Metronome 60 → 65: 4 downs per chord, alternating

**Voice (5 min)**
- [ ] **Up or down?** ear training (Routine 6), 2 minutes
- [ ] Pitch-match all 6 open strings (use octaves as needed). Which ones are easy? Which are hard? Note it
- [ ] Sing along with "Ain't No Sunshine" (just the recording, no guitar), softly, **once**. No pressure, just get the song in your body

**Together (3 min)**
- [ ] Strum + count aloud: Em (4), Am (4), ×4
- [ ] Strum + hum: Am, 4 downs, holding a note

**Week 1 check-in (2 min)**: go through the Week 1 goals list at the top. Tick what you can do.

**Record & Review**
- [ ] Record a short video: Em → Am → Em → Am with 4 downs each, then hum over Am

## Day 7 — Rest (or a light day)
Your fingers and voice consolidate what you practiced during rest. Choose one:

**Option A: Full rest.** No guitar. Listen to "Ain't No Sunshine" 3–5 times, counting "1 2 3 4" along with it. Read [03 — Music Basics](03-Music-Basics-You-Actually-Need.md) fully.

**Option B: Light day (≤15 min).** Tune, Em ↔ Am One-Minute Change once, 5 minutes of lip trills and humming, one sing-along with the record.

**Either way:**
- [ ] Watch your **Day 0 video** and your Day 6 video back to back
- [ ] Write a short **Week 1 reflection** in your log:
  - What got easier?
  - What's still hard?
  - What hurts (if anything)?
  - What's my Em ↔ Am score? My hiss time?

## Week 1 troubleshooting quick hits
| Problem | Quick fix |
|---|---|
| Fingertips really sore | Normal. Shorter sessions, split in two. Stop if blisters start |
| Chord buzzes | Closer to the fret, on your fingertips, more arch |
| One string sounds dead | Another finger is touching it. Pluck-check to find which |
| Pick keeps slipping | Grip slightly firmer, show less pick, try a grippy pick |
| Can't lip trill | Fingers on cheeks; more air; use straw or hum instead for now |
| Can't tell if I'm matching pitch | Use the tuner app on your voice. Slide slowly. Cover one ear |
| Neck/shoulder tight | Posture check; drop your shoulders; don't tilt the guitar back |
